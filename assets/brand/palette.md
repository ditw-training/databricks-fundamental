## How the two systems combine

Altkom `alert-orange #FF5919` sits almost on top of Databricks red `#FF3621`, and Altkom
`midnight-blue #00003C` is a near neighbour of the old `#1E1E2E`. The two identities therefore
overlap rather than clash:

- **Structure** (cover, headers, tables, footer) is Altkom — navy base with electric-blue accents.
- **Technology accent** (the marks that say "this is a Databricks course") stays Databricks red.
- **Warnings** use Altkom's `alert-orange`, keeping red exclusively as a brand signal.
